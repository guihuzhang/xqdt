This directory includes or interfaces with third-party metric implementations used by the paper.

`chrF.py` is derived from Maja Popović's chrF implementation. Its source header states that the program is distributed under the GNU General Public Licence (GPL) and asks publications using the original or a modified version to cite:

> Maja Popović. 2015. *chrF: character n-gram F-score for automatic MT evaluation*. WMT 2015.

The original license notice is preserved in `chrF.py`. No specific GPL version is asserted here beyond what is stated in that source header.
